ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

Training for a changing automotive industry.

National Automotive Technical Institute Excellence Training is presented as a South African technical training institution focused on practical learning, workplace discipline and the development of employable skills.

Our approach combines classroom knowledge with practical exposure so learners can understand how technical principles are applied in real working environments.
